# Best Moving Companies in Connecticut: 7 Options to Compare

Choosing a moving company in Connecticut involves more than finding an available truck. You need a team that can handle your belongings, work around building access, and explain the price before moving day. ## How to Choose a Local Moving Company in Connecticut

For a local move, start with accurate information. Give each company the same inventory, addresses, preferred date, and building-access details.

Mention anything that could affect the work:

- Stairs or limited elevator access.
- Long distances between the truck and entrance.
- Narrow driveways or restricted parking.
- Furniture that needs dismantling.
- Heavy, fragile, or unusually shaped items.
- Additional pickup or delivery stops.

Ask how the estimate is calculated. Confirm crew size, minimum charges, travel time, materials, and any access-related fees.

A dependable local moving company should be able to explain the plan and its assumptions before you book.

## Connecticut Long-Distance and Interstate Moving Companies

A long-distance move needs a clear transport and delivery plan. A move across a state line is interstate even when the mileage is relatively short.

When comparing interstate moving companies serving Connecticut, ask who will transport your belongings and whether you are dealing with the carrier or a broker. FMCSA’s moving guidance emphasizes understanding that distinction and checking registration. [8]

Your written documents should explain the inventory, services, pickup arrangements, delivery window, and charges.

For routes such as Connecticut to New Jersey, Arizona, Colorado, or Washington, confirm availability directly. A general long-distance service listing does not establish that a company can handle your specific route and dates.

### Understand Protection Options

For interstate household moves, FMCSA distinguishes Full Value Protection from Released Value. Released Value generally limits liability to 60 cents per pound per article, which may be far below an item’s actual value. These valuation options are not the same as a separate insurance policy. [9]

Request written terms, exclusions, deductibles, and instructions for declaring valuable items. In-state moves may have different requirements, so do not assume interstate terms apply.

## How Much Do Connecticut Moving Companies Charge?

The cost depends on the inventory, distance, access, dates, packing, storage, and handling requirements. A low headline price is difficult to assess without that detail.

Compare written estimates using the same scope:

| Cost factor | Questions to ask |
|---|---|
| Labor | What crew size, minimum charge, and time assumptions apply? |
| Transport | Are travel, fuel, and route-related charges included? |
| Access | Are stairs, long carries, elevators, or shuttle vehicles extra? |
| Packing | Which materials and labor are included? |
| Specialty items | What requires a separate handling charge? |
| Storage | What are the storage, access, and redelivery fees? |
| Protection | What valuation option is selected, and what does it cost? |
| Changes | How are added items, stops, or date changes priced? |

Request an explanation of the estimate type and circumstances that could change the total.

To make a move more affordable, reduce the inventory before estimates are finalized and ask whether date flexibility affects availability or price.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best moving company in Connecticut?

### Which companies should I contact for an apartment move?

### Can I book packing, moving, and storage together?
